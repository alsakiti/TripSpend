Add Expense:
- Personal: one person's own expense.
  Example: Me paid 10 OMR for Me → no debt.
- Personal paid for another:
  Example: Me paid 10 OMR for Hu → Hu owes Me 10 OMR.
- Shared:
  Example: Me paid 30 OMR for Me + Hu → each share is 15 OMR, so Hu owes Me 15 OMR.

Analytics → Who owes whom only shows real group debts.
