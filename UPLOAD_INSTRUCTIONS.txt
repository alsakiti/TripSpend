TripSpend v6.5 update

1. Extract TripSpend-v6.5-update.zip.
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside it to the root of your existing TripSpend GitHub repository.
3. Replace existing files and commit.
4. Wait about 1–2 minutes.
5. Completely close TripSpend.
6. Reopen it while online.
7. Confirm the visible badge says v6.5.

IMPORTANT:
Your existing v6.4 trip stays as your CURRENT TRIP.
It is not automatically archived or deleted.

New features:
- Settings → Trips & history
- Finish Trip + final summary
- Start New Trip without deleting the old one
- Reopen Past Trips
- Analytics → Who owes whom → Mark Paid
- Settlement history + Undo
- Expenses → Search + Filters
  Category / Personal-Shared / Country / Traveler / Payment / Date range

All v6.4 IndexedDB backups and performance improvements remain.
