TripSpend v6.8.1 update

1. Extract TripSpend-v6.8.1-update.zip.
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside it to the root of your TripSpend GitHub repository.
3. Replace existing files and commit.
4. IMPORTANT: Open Cloudflare > Workers & Pages > tripspend-ai > Edit code.
5. Replace the Worker code with worker/ai-worker.js from this package and Deploy.
6. Keep the Workers AI binding named AI and ALLOWED_ORIGIN=https://alsakiti.github.io.
7. Wait about 1–2 minutes for GitHub Pages.
8. Completely close TripSpend and reopen it while online.
9. Confirm the badge says v6.8.1.
10. Tap the AI button and try: “Add 12 OMR dinner today.” TripSpend must show a confirmation card before saving.

What changed:
- TripSpend AI can prepare and confirm adds/edits for expenses
- AI can prepare and confirm itinerary changes
- AI can add/edit planned costs
- AI can change trip and country budgets
- Every write requires explicit user confirmation
- No AI delete actions in v6.8.1
- Receipt images, backups and past trips are still excluded from AI context
- Existing v6.8.0 dashboard, FX, storage and trip data are preserved
