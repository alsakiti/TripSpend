TripSpend v5.8 update

1. Extract TripSpend-v5.8-update.zip.
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside it to the root of your existing TripSpend GitHub repository.
3. Replace the old files and commit.
4. Wait 1–2 minutes for GitHub Pages.
5. Completely close TripSpend and reopen it while online.
6. Confirm the visible version badge says v5.8.

Home:
- Travelers removed.
- Recent expenses removed.

To see who owes whom:
Analytics → Who owes whom

For settlement to work correctly, each group expense should have:
- Paid by = the person who actually paid
- Expense for = the traveler(s) who shared that expense

TripSpend will then show each person's Paid, Share, Owes/Should receive, plus the exact final payments.
