TripSpend v6.7.6 update

1. Extract TripSpend-v6.7.6-update.zip.
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside it to the root of your TripSpend GitHub repository.
3. Replace existing files and commit.
4. Wait about 1–2 minutes.
5. Completely close TripSpend.
6. Reopen it while online.
7. Confirm the badge says v6.7.6.

What changed:
- Pure Home dashboard redesign
- Budget remains the main hero
- Safe Today + Spent Today combined into one compact strip
- Current Country + Next Up combined into one Trip Context surface
- Country budgets made more compact
- Trip Health and Trips & History reduced in visual weight
- Plan remains the only full itinerary/planning screen
- Accessible in-app country budget editor retained
- No data, IndexedDB, or calculation migration
