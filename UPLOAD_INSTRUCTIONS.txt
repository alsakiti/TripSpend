TripSpend v4.4 update

1. Extract TripSpend-v4.4-update.zip.
2. Upload every file/folder inside to the ROOT of your existing TripSpend GitHub repository.
3. Replace the old files and commit.
4. Wait 1–2 minutes for GitHub Pages.
5. Completely close the Home Screen app and reopen it online.
6. Confirm you see v4.4 under the TripSpend logo.

Changes: improved iPhone date picker appearance and blank name/destination defaults.
