TripSpend v5.2 update

1. Extract TripSpend-v5.2-update.zip.
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside it to the root of your existing TripSpend GitHub repository.
3. Replace the old files and commit.
4. Wait 1–2 minutes.
5. Completely close the Home Screen app.
6. Reopen it while online and confirm the badge says v5.2.

New Trip:
- Choose the first destination normally.
- Tap + Add another country if the trip visits more places.
- Add the next country, its From/To dates and local currency.
- Repeat as needed.
- Tap Create Trip.

Existing trip:
Settings → + Add another country
or
Home → Trip plan → Open planner
