TripSpend v6.1 update

1. Extract TripSpend-v6.1-update.zip.
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside it to the root of your existing TripSpend GitHub repository.
3. Replace the old files and commit.
4. Wait about 1–2 minutes.
5. Completely close the TripSpend Home Screen app.
6. Reopen it while online.
7. Confirm the visible badge says v6.1.

What changed:
- Current country switches automatically from the trip dates.
- Add Expense is much shorter: Amount → Personal/Shared → Save.
- Tap AUTO-FILLED or More options only when you need to change details.
- Analytics → Who owes whom now shows the final payments first.
- Tap Show calculation details only if you want Paid vs Share accounting.
