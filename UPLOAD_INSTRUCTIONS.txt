TripSpend v5.9.1 update

1. Extract TripSpend-v5.9.1-update.zip.
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside it to the root of your existing TripSpend GitHub repository.
3. Replace the old files and commit.
4. Wait 1–2 minutes for GitHub Pages.
5. Completely close TripSpend and reopen it online.
6. Confirm the visible version badge says v5.9.1.

Personal expense behavior:
- Me paid for Me → counts toward trip/country spending, creates no debt.
- Me paid for Hu → counts toward trip/country spending, Hu owes Me.
- Shared expense → counts toward trip/country spending and is split between selected travelers.

The Add Expense Date control is now compact and matches the other date fields.
